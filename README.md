### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a pragmatist Software Architect and Senior Software Engineer with a 15-year track record of designing, scaling, and modernizing mission-critical enterprise systems. My engineering philosophy revolves around first-principles problem solving, identifying root performance bottlenecks, and building clean, zero-dependency architectures. 

- 🏢 Currently architecting a national-scale, high-throughput platform at **Avash (National Tax Administration Project)**.
- ⚙️ Previously led architectural turnarounds, CDC data pipelines, and microservices migrations at **Mabna Card Aria**, **Sharif Hamrah Pazhohan**, and major banking institutions.
- 📐 Specializing in **Domain-Driven Design (DDD)**, **Clean Architecture**, and **Monolith Decomposition**.
- 🛠️ Deep expertise in **C# (.NET)**, **Java (Spring Boot)**, and cross-platform mobile ecosystems (**Flutter / Native Android**).
- 💬 Ask me about: **Distributed Systems, Event-Driven Architecture, Memory Profiling, or Server-Driven UI (SDUI)**.
